# Dashboard and app surfaces

Scope: signed-in dashboard, board view dialog, link form; the same world carries to landing, auth, profile and public boards.
Visitor mode: Operate. Audience: anyone collecting and sharing link boards. Task: find, open, add and organise links; share boards.
Constraints: one light mode only (user, 2026-11-24); colour identity from the logo blues (indigo #4632E6, blue #3B82F6, sky #68C8F2); keep the logo files; standard web controls.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Boards are sheets in a well-kept notebook. Refuses the category default of a white app with a grey sidebar and one blue accent.
OWN-WORLD: Pale blue graph-paper ground with a whisper-faint 24px grid; an indigo cover band as the header; white ruled sheets with soft blue rules; ballpoint-navy ink; logo blue and sky for counts and accents; one red, rationed to Public and destructive actions. One friendly geometric sans (Figtree), heavy for titles, regular for rows.
STORY: The visitor sees all their boards at once, scans titles and link counts, opens a link in one click, and adds or shares without hunting.
